# Mastery Standard

A course is not complete because lectures were watched.

## Universal exit standard
Every module ends in a portfolio artifact and an oral/code-ownership review.

### Mathematics / statistics
- closed-book derivation of central results;
- numerical or simulation implementation;
- comparison with a trusted library;
- assumptions, numerical error and sensitivity analysis;
- oral defense.

### CS / systems / platform
- design document;
- working implementation or deployment;
- automated tests;
- profiler/benchmark where relevant;
- deliberate failure injection and recovery;
- debugging/postmortem notes;
- code-ownership interview, including AI-assisted code.

### Security
- only owned, explicitly authorized or deliberately vulnerable lab targets;
- threat model;
- attack/reproduction evidence;
- relevant telemetry/detection;
- mitigation;
- clean retest;
- written report and oral defense.

### ML / AI
- baseline hierarchy;
- dataset/data card;
- reproducible configs;
- held-out evaluation;
- at least two meaningful ablations;
- calibration/failure/error analysis where relevant;
- latency, memory and cost profile;
- model/system card;
- oral defense.

### Research
- explicit research question;
- literature map;
- reproducible experiment package;
- statistical analysis and negative results;
- limitations;
- paper-style report;
- adversarial technical review.

## AI-use rule
AI may propose and implement. The learner approves only after independently testing, understanding and defending the result.
